Bengali Food

Bengali cuisine is known for fish, rice and aromatic spices.

Gujarati Food

Gujarati cuisine is mostly vegetarian and famous for sweet and spicy flavors.

Mughlai Food

Mughlai cuisine includes rich dishes like biryani and kebabs.

Punjabi Food

Punjabi cuisine is famous for butter chicken, naan and tandoori dishes.

South Indian Cuisine

South Indian cuisine includes dosa, idli and sambar.
