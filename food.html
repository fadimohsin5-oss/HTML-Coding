English Cuisine

Traditional English food is known for its hearty meals and comforting flavours. Classic dishes include fish and chips, full English breakfast, roast dinners and traditional pies. These meals are popular across the UK and are often enjoyed with family and friends.

Fish and Chips

Fish and chips is one of the most famous British dishes. It consists of battered fish deep fried until crispy and served with thick cut chips. It is traditionally served with salt, vinegar and mushy peas.

Full English Breakfast

A full English breakfast usually includes eggs, bacon, sausages, baked beans, mushrooms and toast. It is a very filling meal often eaten in the morning.

Sunday Roast

Sunday roast is a traditional British meal usually eaten on Sundays. It includes roast meat, potatoes, vegetables, Yorkshire pudding and gravy.

Shepherd's Pie

Shepherd's pie is a classic comfort dish made with minced lamb, vegetables and gravy topped with mashed potatoes and baked until golden.
